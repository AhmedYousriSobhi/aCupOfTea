# Correlation

> Two pages on columns that move together. By the end you'll know how to spot features that say the same thing twice, and what to do about extreme cases.

Correlation just means "when one column goes up, the other tends to go up (or down) too". It's useful when you want to understand your data. It's a problem when two of your features are nearly copies of each other: the model can't tell which one deserves the credit. That's multicollinearity.
