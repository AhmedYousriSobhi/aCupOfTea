The shirt that shimmers: filter the spectrum, then sample it

Filtering in frequency is three steps: transform, multiply by a mask, transform back. Cut too sharply and it rings; sample too coarsely and it aliases.

What this assumes
The picture is made up in code (128 × 128) and padded with zeros to 256 × 256 before the transform, then cropped: this avoids the wraparound of circular convolution. The ringing strip is a 256-sample pulse, 128 samples wide, filtered with the same mask as a function of distance from the centre. A wave sampled at exactly twice its frequency is a borderline case and is not shown. Nothing here is random.
