Spread the numbers out: rescue the dark X-ray

An intensity transform is a lookup table. Equalization builds it from the running total of the histogram: s = (L − 1) · cumulative share.

Where does the darkest crowd go?

Equalization sends each gray level to (L − 1) times the share of pixels at or below it.

What this assumes
Gray levels are whole numbers, 8 bits for the picture and 3 bits for the bag. Equalization rounds to the nearest level (halves round up). The scene is made up in code, then darkened to a quarter and rounded. Log uses s = 255 · ln(1 + r) / ln 256, so 0 stays 0 and 255 stays 255. Everything on this page is exact; nothing is random.
