What this assumes
Values are whole numbers, so boundaries are the limits plus or minus 0.5. The grouped mean stands every value in a class at its midpoint; the grouped median assumes values spread evenly inside a class. Both are estimates, and the error depends on where the class edges fall, not only on the width. The 50-day data are kept as counts only, so no raw mean or median exists to compare.
