Who you ask matters more than how many

Five ways to pick 40 customers from 4,000. Repeat each a thousand times and see which ones find the truth, which are merely noisy, and which are confidently wrong.

What this assumes
An invented crowd with fixed shares per group (70%, 50% and 25%), seeded draws. The doorstep poll draws keen customers nine times as often as the others. Systematic sampling steps through a list whose pattern repeats every 20 customers, so a step of 100 always lands on the same kind of person; the shuffled list has no pattern. A cluster is a block of 100 neighbours of one kind, and the number of clusters is n / 10.

Part of Probability & Statistics, One Sip at a Time. Invented customers, seeded random draws.
