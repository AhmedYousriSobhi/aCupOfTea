Guided experiments

Each button sets the dials and draws a sample. Predict, then compare the bars with the blue ticks.

  • 2 and 3: the black line is Poisson(np). It tracks the bars only when n is large and p small.
  • 4: the shaded bar at 0 is e−4, about 1.8%.
  • 5: the shaded area is the 95.45% of the 68-95-99.7 rule.
  • Any recipe: draw several samples. The sample mean and variance wobble around the theory values.
Assumptions. Binomial: same p, independent tries. Poisson: constant rate, independent arrivals (variance should be near the mean). Normal: draw the histogram first; two humps or a long tail are not a bell.
