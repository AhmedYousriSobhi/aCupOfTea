# Data Science in Business - Overview

> A tour of the businesses a data scientist ends up working for: what they do, which numbers they watch, and which projects keep showing up.

You join a new company on Monday. By Wednesday someone says "our conversion is down" and everyone nods. Do you know what conversion means *here*? Domain knowledge is what turns a model into something the business will use.

Data scientists work close to the business, whatever the company focuses on. These pages come from that experience. Each one highlights the company's target KPIs and the projects a data scientist could take on.

## Who this is for

Anyone about to work with, or interview for, one of these domains. Some projects repeat across companies, so pick one and build it: that is good practice.
