# Programming - Overview

> The programming fundamentals behind AI and data science work: ten small corners, from Python itself to how your OS handles memory. Pick the one that matches your current headache.

Your script works on your laptop. Then it runs out of memory, or crawls on ten times the data, or a teammate can't read it, or you can't find the commit that broke it. None of that is a modeling problem. It's a programming-fundamentals problem, and this section is where those fundamentals live.
